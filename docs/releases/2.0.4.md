# Halcyon 2.0.4 "Short Sword"

The last of the 2.0 line: a sword art of its own, the themed pickers grown into workspace tools, and a desktop that
asks the graphics card for less.

- **Sword Art Online**, a new theme: Aincrad at dusk, and the game's main menu, drawn from scratch, in place of the
  overview. A column of round buttons drops in one after another; the chosen one's panel unfolds beside it: Status
  (HP is the battery), Items (the apps by category), Party (open windows), Map (workspaces as floors), Messages
  (notifications) and System, whose Log Out asks with the game's ○ and ✕. The animated *Twilight Aincrad* wallpaper
  (fan art, via MotionBGs) is fetched when the theme is applied.
- **The manga book** has a table of contents and chapters by app type; pages can be dragged across the book; and a
  double-click under the last row of apps drops a tablet with every workspace: click an app to go to it, Shift + drag
  it to move it to another workspace.
- **The After ICEY phone** has a fifth app, Spaces, which unfolds it like a foldable into a second screen with the
  same workspace board.
- **HyperNeo** gets a new clock (a smoked-glass widget whose neon ring fills with the seconds), a new revolver (a
  smoked disc in an ember-rose-violet ring) and a new dock and top bar (smoked glass in a neon edge).
- **Manga Ink's top bar** is square: no pills or rounded dividers, in both Manga Ink themes. The dock's pin button
  sits inside the dock in every theme.
- **Window resistance**: a dragged window stops for a moment at another window's edge or the screen's edge
  (hyprland-plus's `plus:window_resistance`; Hyprland's snapping otherwise), and with Halcyon's monitor wall a window
  arriving on another monitor gives that edge a small bulge.
- **Less work for the GPU**: animations that redrew the bar, dock or panels every frame while nothing changed now
  rest (ICEY's scan, Silence's eyes, the update sparkles, wavy progress bars, a hidden cookie clock); animated
  wallpapers play at their own frame rate instead of the screen's; motion blur takes 3 samples; fullscreen games
  scan out directly; and hyprland-plus skips motion blur on moves too small to see.
- **hyprland-plus 0.57.0.p3**: window resistance, adaptive motion blur, and touchD's `touchdctl doctor`,
  `profile auto` and `calibrate --apply`.
- **Fixes**: icon packs for HyperNeo, Star Rail, the glass themes, Red Sun and Silence failed to build (a palette
  of three colours was read as two).
