# Halcyon 2.0.1 "Weathered Copper"

Copper, after its first weather: fixes and finish on 2.0.

- **Loaf is the file manager.** Super + E opens [Loaf](https://github.com/m-uvex/loaf-fm) (`loaf-fm`, installed with
  the other dependencies on Arch); folders open in it and it takes Dolphin's place in the dock and launcher pins, once
  it is installed. `halcyon files use dolphin` goes back.
- **Windows no longer vanish behind a terminal.** A maximised tiled window hides the other tiles with nothing left to
  click, so a terminal seemed to swallow them until it was closed. Hypr mode turns itself off after 10 seconds
  without one of its keys (it could be left on and eat what you typed: plain M maximised), its maximise is Shift + M,
  and a maximised window over other tiles now gets a notification with a button that brings them back.
- **Manga Ink is one manga page.** It is a light theme now: the top bar, the side panels, the wallpaper cards and the
  dock are all paper panels in thick ink borders, with hard shadows and screentone, like the dock was. Manga Ink Dark
  is its negative.
- **The cheat sheet has a Hypr keys page**: the Hyper chords and hypr mode's keys on a page of their own, with how to
  use them; the Keybinds page no longer mixes them in.
- Tests no longer touch the real machine (pacman, a hyprland-plus in /usr/local/bin).
