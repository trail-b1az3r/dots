# Halcyon 2.0.0 "Copper"

The first named release. Halcyon's versions take their names from metals, softest first: 2.0 is **Copper**, 2.1 will
be **Brass**, and 2.2 **Bronze**.

## Themes

- **Two new themes.** **Silence**: black and crimson, a face in the dark, with a still wallpaper fetched when the theme
  is first applied. **Manga Ink Dark**: Manga Ink in negative, its wallpaper inverted on your computer with the colours
  kept.
- **Every theme has its own look everywhere**: the dock, the top bar, the AI panel and the quick panel take the
  theme's frame; After ICEY, Manga Ink (and Dark) and Silence have revolvers and clocks of their own.
- **Icon packs** for After ICEY, Manga Ink and Manga Ink Dark, made from the icons you use, so every app is covered.
- **A theme picker** (Ctrl + Super + Shift + T): cards with each theme's wallpaper, colours and frame.
- **A new theme while you are away**: after some idle minutes, the next, a random or one chosen theme, put back when
  you return.
- **Theme switch rules**: change widgets, icons and settings when the theme switches to or away from a theme.
- The bar's **sun dial** turns over at night and shows the moon in tonight's phase.

## Windows

- **The wall between monitors** (with hyprland-plus): a dragged window stops at the edge of another monitor, which
  bulges where it would come in; push on and it goes through.
- **Super + drag, then switch workspace**: the window comes along.
- **Golden ratio** is the golden spiral now, and Golden Ratio Plus keeps the main window at 61.8% without shrinking
  the rest away. Two-at-a-time and Mondrian placements.
- Hyper + Ctrl + Tab brings the next floating window to the top; mini windows for every window; minimise all.
- Drop a window at the top of the screen to fullscreen it.

## The desktop

- The power button opens the **power menu**, with **power levels** (power saver, balanced, performance).
- A better **lock screen** (hyprlock) and **idle** (the screen dims before it locks).
- Widgets: audio visualizer, system, now playing, lyrics, weather (no jq needed), calendar events and alarms.
- Avizo and Wayscriber in the theme's colours; Firefox themed on every site; theme-coloured notifications.

## Fixes and tools

- **Sound and webcams after KDE**: EasyEffects no longer starts by itself, KDE's leftovers are cleared at login, and
  the microphone menu stops flickering. `halcyon audio` explains and fixes.
- **Wi-Fi at login** (iwd kept, NetworkManager installed when missing); `halcyon wifi`, `halcyon doctor`.
- **`halcyon update`** updates Halcyon and hyprland-plus; hyprland-plus really installs into /usr/local/bin.
- **HyperNix T1**: T1 and T2 keys both work and can be pasted in Settings; keyless when the server trusts you.
- **Assistant voices**: any Piper voice (GLaDOS included), Kokoro, Fish Audio S2 Pro, drama3-small, your own program.
- **NixOS**: a real install path, as a system module.
- Settings: one window at a time, on your monitor; every section folds.

## Next

- **2.2 Bronze**: a normal Hyprland plugin with hyprland-plus's additions, as an alternative to the fork, so they
