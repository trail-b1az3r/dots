# Halcyon 2.0.2 "Oxidized Copper"

Copper gone green: the themes get app pickers of their own.

- **Manga Ink's app picker is a manga book.** Super opens it: the ink cover swings open over the spine, and the apps
  are printed on its pages, sixteen to a page. Click the turned-up corner or the outer margin of the right page to
  turn on, of the left page to turn back (or use the arrows, Page Up and Down, the wheel); each page turns over the
  spine. Typing searches every app, and a speech bubble says what Enter will do: open an app, give a sum, run a
  command. Esc shuts the book before it goes. Manga Ink Dark prints the same book in negative.
- **After ICEY's app picker is a phone.** Super slides it up: a chamfered navy phone with a cyan rim, its clock, and
  four apps. **Apps** lists every app with a search; **Auto** takes anything you type and works out what you meant
  (an app opens, a sum is worked out, a command runs, or it searches the web); **Theme** shows the Halcyon themes in
  their own colours, tap one to wear it; **Calculator** is a keypad worked out by qalc. Typing on the home screen goes
  straight to Auto. Esc goes back home, then puts the phone away.
- Both are on by default and can be turned off in **Settings > Halcyon > On the screen** ("The theme's own app
  picker"). Off, and in every other theme, Super opens the overview as before.
